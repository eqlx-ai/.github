# eqlx

**AI for work that takes expertise.** [eqlx.ai](https://eqlx.ai) · İstanbul

eqlx builds AI for work that takes expertise, for financial institutions and fintechs, and for manufacturers and service companies:

- **[Machine-learning models](https://eqlx.ai/ml-models)** that estimate: scores, probabilities, rankings, forecasts, and the action each band triggers inside the institution's thresholds.
- **[Small language models](https://eqlx.ai/small-language-models)** trained for one domain and task: they read and reason like your expert, call the right tool, and write in your format.
- **[Agentic AI systems](https://eqlx.ai/agentic-ai)** around them that prepare each case and act only inside the limits the institution writes.

Each layer works on its own; they stack where the work needs more. Everything runs in the customer's environment and is handed over running, and the institution's experts keep the decision.
